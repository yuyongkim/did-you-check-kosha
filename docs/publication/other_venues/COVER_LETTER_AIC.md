Dear Editor,

I am writing to submit the above-titled manuscript for consideration for publication in *Automation in Construction*.

**Scope and relevance.** This paper presents an integrated AI verification platform for seven engineering disciplines used across the full lifecycle of EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) process plant projects—from design verification through construction commissioning to in-service inspection. The work directly addresses the scope of *Automation in Construction* by automating engineering calculation verification, cross-discipline hazard detection, and regulatory compliance checking that are currently performed through fragmented, manual workflows in plant engineering practice.

**Practical engineering significance.** The paper addresses two gaps with direct consequences for EPC project execution:

1. **Cross-discipline coupling hazards** — Single-domain tools used in EPC projects cannot detect hazards arising from discipline interfaces (piping nozzle loads affecting rotating equipment, foundation settlement propagating into alignment failure). Our cross-discipline validator increases hazard blocking from 0 to 25/60 scenarios (+41.7%) in ablation testing.

2. **Jurisdiction compliance gaps** — In Korean EPC projects, engineering calculations satisfying ASME/API/IEEE codes may still fail to meet KOSHA PSM regulatory obligations. We demonstrate this empirically: in three cases where international codes fully pass, the KOSHA RAG layer identifies Korean-jurisdiction requirements including Article 256 corrosion-prevention obligations. This has direct implications for PSM audits, inspection planning, and regulatory compliance in operating plants.

**Key contributions:**

- First integrated platform verifying seven process plant disciplines (piping, vessels, rotating, electrical, instrumentation, steel, civil) against both international codes and Korean KOSHA PSM regulations.
- Four-layer verification model applied to real EPC engineering standards (16 major standards).
- KOSHA regulatory RAG layer with 16,174 searchable provisions enabling automatic jurisdiction compliance gap detection.
- 220-case golden dataset, 60-scenario ablation, and 50-query retrieval benchmark with publicly released code and data.

**Industry relevance.** The author brings 12+ years of professional experience in petrochemical EPC and process plant engineering, ensuring that the system architecture and evaluation scenarios reflect actual plant engineering practice rather than hypothetical constructs. The jurisdiction compliance gap concept generalizes to any jurisdiction with national regulations layered on international codes (EU PED, Saudi GACA, Chinese GB standards).

**Reproducibility.** All source code, datasets, and benchmark scripts are publicly released under the AGPL-3.0 license.

**Confirmations.** This manuscript has not been published previously, is not under consideration elsewhere, and there are no conflicts of interest.

I believe this work will be of substantial interest to the *Automation in Construction* readership, particularly researchers and practitioners working on AI-assisted engineering verification, plant asset management, and regulatory automation in the construction and process industries.

Dear Editor,

I am writing to submit the above-titled manuscript for consideration for publication in *Expert Systems with Applications*.

**Scope and relevance.** This paper presents an integrated AI verification platform that combines seven domain-specific calculation engines (piping, pressure vessels, rotating equipment, electrical, instrumentation, structural steel, and civil structures), a four-layer hybrid verification model, and a regulatory retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) layer grounded in the Korean Occupational Safety and Health Agency (KOSHA) regulatory corpus. The system directly addresses the scope of *Expert Systems with Applications* as an expert system that encodes domain knowledge from sixteen international engineering standards (ASME, API, IEEE, IEC, ACI, AISC) and integrates jurisdiction-specific regulatory intelligence to automate compliance verification across the full plant lifecycle.

**Key contributions.** The paper makes four contributions:

1. **Seven-discipline integrated verification architecture** that automatically detects cross-discipline coupling hazards across ten predefined domain pairs—a capability absent from existing single-domain engineering tools.

2. **KOSHA regulatory RAG layer** indexing 16,174 searchable rows from 1,327 KOSHA guide documents and 3,102 statutory provisions, representing the first systematic integration of Korean PSM regulations into an engineering AI system.

3. **Quantitative evaluation framework** comprising 220 synthetic golden cases (100% pass), a 60-scenario cross-discipline ablation (+25 blocked scenarios when validator enabled, +41.7%), and a 50-query retrieval benchmark (Recall@1 improvement from 0.44 to 0.74).

4. **Jurisdiction compliance gap concept** — empirically demonstrated through three case studies showing that international code calculations can fully pass while Korean regulatory obligations remain unaddressed, including Article 256 of the Rules on Occupational Safety and Health Standards.

**Novelty.** To our knowledge, no prior work has: (a) integrated KOSHA regulatory data into engineering AI verification, (b) applied K-voting consensus verification to plant engineering calculation services, or (c) operationalized the concept of jurisdiction compliance gaps between international engineering codes and national safety regulations.

**Reproducibility.** Source code, golden datasets, and benchmark scripts are publicly released under the AGPL-3.0 license on GitHub, with a detailed code-to-paper mapping document.

**Confirmations.** This manuscript has not been published previously, is not under consideration for publication elsewhere, and its publication has been approved by all authors. There are no conflicts of interest to declare.

I believe this work will be of significant interest to the readership of *Expert Systems with Applications*, particularly those working on domain-specific AI systems, regulatory compliance automation, and engineering knowledge systems.
